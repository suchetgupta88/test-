Questions & answers

Common questions about Friday delivery.

What if I have an unusual letterbox or live in a flat? +

Our flat packaging measures 235mm × 165mm × 32mm. It is engineered specifically to meet Royal Mail and universal postal standards for letter-slot delivery. If you have a gated entrance or shared lobby, you can provide gate code instructions during your initial account setup.

Can I pause if I go away on holiday? +

Yes. You can pause for 1 to 8 weeks, skip an upcoming Friday, or switch to fortnightly at any time. Just tap the one-click manage link in any weekly email by Wednesday 12:00 PM.

How long does the coffee stay fresh? +

Because we roast on Wednesday and ship with a one-way degassing valve, the beans hit their peak aromatic plateau between day 3 and day 21 post-roast. Kept in the zip-sealed pouch in a cool, dark cupboard, they remain vibrant for 4 to 6 weeks.
